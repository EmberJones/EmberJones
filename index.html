About

Hi, I'm Ember Jones, a programmer and game developer based in Cape Town, South Africa. I’m completing my BCIS in Game Design and Development at Vega and am open to work opportunities.

Proficient in C# and C++, with experience in Unity and Unreal Engine, I enjoy collaborating with artists, composers and designers to bring shared ideas to life. I make silly, light-hearted games with offbeat mechanics, and I’m especially interested in procedural generation and storytelling through gameplay.

Work

2Skate

2026 Physics Game Jam · 7 days · with Craig Harrod, Kaellum Alves and Devilliers Cloete

A physics-based rollerskating simulator where you control each skate separately, the left on WASD and the right on the arrow keys, while a ragdoll body flops along on top. I focused on programming the movement of each rollerskate and on setting up and debugging the character’s collisions with the environment. I also fixed several major bugs involving clipping, gliding and force application.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Ragdoll physics
  • Wheel colliders
  • Play-in-browser

The Campus Game Jam 2026 · ~50 hours · with Devilliers Cloete and Jonty de Longchamps

A satirical first-person bullet-hell about reflecting the bullets that corporate workers fire at you. I focused on the movement mechanics, the time-slow combat mechanic and its visual effects, and parts of the level layout such as enemy placements and respawn locations.
